FAQ

Questions, answered

Is AlagoBets really free?

Yes. You join through our free plan on Winible, then hop into the Discord. There is nothing to pay to join the community. A VIP tier is in the works and will be announced in Discord first.

How do the Discord models work?

Each model is a Discord bot that only responds to commands. You type a slash command such as /nflslate or /nbaprops and the bot replies with its read. Nothing gets auto-posted; you decide what to look at and what to bet, if anything.

Which sports do you cover?

NFL, NBA, MLB and NHL each have their own model, plus a Props model for player props. The community also talks NCAAF, tennis and more.

Who can join?

AlagoBets is for adults 21 and older. Please check that sports betting is legal where you live before you place any bet.

Are wins guaranteed?

No. Nobody can guarantee results, and past wins don't predict future ones. Treat everything here as information and entertainment, bet only what you can afford to lose, and never chase losses.

Where can I get help with gambling?

If gambling stops being fun, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) for free, confidential support, 24/7.
