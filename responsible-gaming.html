Responsible gaming

Keep it fun. Keep it safe.

AlagoBets is a free community for adults 21 and older. Our models give information, not guarantees. Every bet carries risk, and no model or person can promise a win.

Need help now? Call or text 1‑800‑GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) for free, confidential support, 24/7. You can also visit ncpgambling.org.

Healthy habits

  • Set a budget before you bet and stick to it.
  • Only bet money you can afford to lose. Never bet rent, bills or borrowed money.
  • Don't chase losses. Losing streaks happen to everyone.
  • Set time limits and take breaks.
  • Don't bet when upset, tired or under the influence.
  • Check that sports betting is legal where you live.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned, or more to feel the same excitement.
  • Hiding betting from family or friends.
  • Borrowing money or selling things to bet.
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when you're not betting.

Tools that help

Most sportsbooks and exchanges offer deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion in your account settings. Many states also run self-exclusion programs.
